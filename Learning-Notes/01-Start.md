# **Module 1: Start Your Cyber Security Journey** 

_TryHackMe notes - the intro module, no hacking yet just the basics_ 

This module is basically the intro, no hands-on hacking yet, just getting your head around what cybersecurity actually is and why it matters. Good to skim even if you think you already know this stuff. 

## **The main idea: CIA Triad** 

Everything in security kind of boils down to protecting these 3 things: 

- Confidentiality - keeping data secret from people who shouldn't see it (encryption, access control) 

- Integrity - making sure data hasn't been tampered with (hashing, checksums) 

- Availability - making sure systems/data are actually accessible when needed (this is why DDoS attacks are a thing, they target availability) 

Basically every attack you'll learn about breaks one (or more) of these three. 

## **Key terms (kept mixing these up at first)** 

- Vulnerability - a weakness in a system (e.g. outdated software, misconfig) 

- Threat - something/someone that could exploit a vulnerability 

- Exploit - the actual method/code used to take advantage of a vulnerability 

- Risk - basically likelihood x impact, i.e. how bad would it be if this vulnerability got exploited 

## **Red team vs Blue team vs Purple team** 

- Red team = offensive, they attack systems to find weaknesses (pentesters, ethical hackers) 

- Blue team = defensive, they defend and monitor (SOC analysts, incident response) 

- Purple team = the two working together, sharing findings to improve defences 

Most beginner content (and most of TryHackMe tbh) leans red team / offensive because it's more fun to learn that way, but the blue side is just as important and honestly probably where more of the jobs are. 

## **Why this matters (career-wise)** 

This module also points you toward other TryHackMe paths depending on what you're into - offensive pentesting, cyber defense, SOC analyst stuff etc. Worth having a rough idea of which direction interests you before grinding rooms randomly.
